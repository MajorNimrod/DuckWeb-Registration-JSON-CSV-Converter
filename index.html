How it works

This tool converts your DuckWeb registration data into a spreadsheet-friendly CSV, and can combine multiple people’s schedules into a when2meet-style availability grid.

1. Export from DuckWeb
  1. Open your weekly schedule / registration page
  2. Press F12 → Network tab
  3. Refresh page, filter by Fetch/XHR
  4. Right-click the JSON request → “Save all as HAR with content…”
2. Convert here
  1. Select your .har or raw .json
  2. Click Convert to CSV to add a member
  3. Repeat for each person, then scroll to see group availability
